How billing works

  • Prices are in US dollars (USD), per month or per year (yearly = 10 times the monthly price: you save 2 months). Depending on your country, taxes (VAT or equivalent) may be added at checkout and are shown before you pay.
  • The subscription renews automatically every month or every year, depending on the period you chose, until you cancel. You can cancel at any time in the app and you will not be charged again; you keep the plan until the end of the paid period.
  • 7 days free on Fluye Pro for customers (US$7) and Fluye Pro for businesses (US$10), monthly or yearly. One trial per account and per device; Verified Plus has no trial. Nothing is charged during the trial; the first charge is on day 8 to the card or account you added, unless you cancel before. We remind you by notification and email 1 day before it ends.
  • Payment methods: credit or debit card, PayPal and, where your device supports it, Apple Pay or Google Pay.
  • Fluye Partner price: businesses that accept Fluye Points pay 15% less for Fluye Pro (US$8.50/month or US$85/year) while they accept them.
  • First payment: if it’s not for you, we refund you within 14 days. See the refund policy.
