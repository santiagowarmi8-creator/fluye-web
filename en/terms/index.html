1. What Fluye is

Fluye is a platform to book appointments at independent businesses (salons, barbershops, spas, clinics, labs and others) in Latin America. Each business is responsible for its services, prices, staff, hours and service. Fluye does not provide beauty or health services.

2. Your account

You must be 18 or older and give truthful information. You are responsible for keeping your password safe and for what is done with your account.

3. Bookings, deposits and cancellations

  • Some businesses ask for a deposit to hold the time. If you pay by bank transfer, the slot is held for a limited time while the business confirms the payment; if it isn’t confirmed, it is released.
  • You can cancel or move your appointment in the app. Each business decides on deposit refunds under its own policy, shown before you book.
  • Service prices are set by each business in its local currency.
  • If you don’t show up several times, the business may ask you for a deposit on future bookings.

4. Plans and subscriptions
