07 Frequently asked questions

What people ask us most.

How much does it cost for customers?

Creating your account and booking is free. You pay the business for the service, as always. You already earn Fluye Points on your appointments; with Fluye Pro for customers (US$7 a month) you earn double plus exclusive bonuses.

What are Fluye Points?

Everyone earns points for every completed appointment (the exchange rate depends on your country's currency); Pro members earn double and exclusive referral bonuses. Points can be redeemed for discounts from 500 points.

Where is it available?

Across Latin America: you pick your country when you create your account (Mexico, Colombia, Argentina, Chile, Peru, the Dominican Republic and more). Each business uses its own time zone and currency. You can use Fluye from your browser at appfluye.app or install the Android app.

How do I install the app on Android?

Download the APK file, open it and allow “install apps from this source” if your phone asks. It will be on Google Play soon.

Does Fluye charge for my appointments?

No. Payment is between you and the business: at the venue or by transfer to their account. Fluye does not store card data.

What happens if my business reaches 50 appointments in a month?

Your customers see your WhatsApp to coordinate, and the quota resets the next month. With Pro there is no limit.

Can I cancel Fluye Pro?

Yes, anytime from the dashboard. You keep Pro until the end of the paid period and then go back to the free plan without losing your data.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Delete my account. You can also see how here.
