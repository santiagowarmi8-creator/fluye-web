02 Payments

Pay the way the business accepts. Straight to them.

Just three clear options: cash on arrival, bank transfer with a receipt, or PayPal. Each business picks which ones it accepts, and Fluye never touches your money.

  • CashPay at the shop when you arrive, as always.
  • Bank transferSee the business’s account with copy buttons and upload your receipt in the app.
  • PayPalTap “Pay with PayPal” and the money goes straight to the business’s PayPal account. When its currency allows it, the appointment confirms by itself.
  • No Fluye commissionFluye takes no commission on your appointments and never stores your card or bank details.

09 FAQ

What people ask us most.

How much does it cost for customers?

Creating your account and booking is free. You pay the business for the service, as always. With Fluye Pro for customers (US$7 a month) you earn double points and exclusive bonuses.

How do I pay for my appointment?

The way the business accepts it: cash at the shop, bank transfer (we show their account and you upload the receipt) or PayPal. Each business shows its accepted methods on its profile.

Does Fluye keep my money?

No. With PayPal the payment goes straight to the business’s PayPal account; with a transfer, to its bank. Fluye takes no commission on appointments and never stores card details or passwords.

What does the free business plan include?

50 bookings a month, 1 professional, up to 15 services, 1 location, calendar, profile, reviews, basic notifications, stories, in-store QR, vacation mode and cash, transfer and PayPal payments.

What do I get with Fluye Pro?

Unlimited bookings, a team of up to 30, commissions and time off, products, home service, gift cards, analytics, day close in PDF, Excel, promotions and waitlist. Try it free for 7 days; monthly or yearly (2 months free).

Where is it available?

Across Latin America. You pick your country when you sign up and each business uses its own currency and time zone. Use Fluye in your browser at appfluye.app or install the Android app.

How do I install the Android app?

Tap “Download the app”, open the APK file and allow “install apps from this source” if your phone asks. On iPhone, open appfluye.app in Safari and add it to your Home Screen.

How do I cancel a plan?

In the app, under Profile → Plan. You keep what you paid for until the end of the period. If you cancel during the 7-day trial, nothing is charged. Refund policy.
