Privacy policy

Fluye (“Fluye”, “we”), a brand of Warmi Marcelo Peralta Santiago (individual, Santiago de los Caballeros, Dominican Republic), runs the Fluye Android app, the web version at appfluye.app and related services. This policy explains what personal data we process, why, and what rights you have. Fluye is offered across Latin America: our baseline is the Dominican Republic’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 172-13 (the country the service is run from), and we also respect the rights your own country’s data protection law gives you, such as accessing, correcting, deleting or objecting to the use of your data.

1. Data we collect

  • Account: name, email and/or mobile number and password (stored only as a bcrypt hash; we never see it). If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google name, email and profile photo.
  • Appointments: business, service, professional, date and time, attendee name, notes, chosen payment method, bank and, if you upload it, the transfer receipt.
  • Health (optional): the name of your health insurer (ARS) to show clinics that accept it. We don’t ask for diagnoses or medical history.
  • Location (optional): if you allow it, your approximate location to sort nearby businesses. Businesses can pin their address on the map.
  • Photos: the ones you upload (for example of your business, services or receipts). We strip metadata (EXIF/GPS) when we receive them.
  • Notifications: a device identifier to send you push notifications, if you turn them on.
  • Businesses: business, team, hours and services data, bank accounts the business publishes to receive transfers, and the plan payment history (we don’t store card numbers).
  • Technical and security data: IP address, server logs, an anonymous install identifier and hashed email and phone fingerprints to prevent fraud and abuse of the free plan.
  • Flo assistant: the options you pick in the assistant menu, to continue the conversation.

We don’t show ads or use advertising identifiers. We don’t sell your data.

2. Why we use it

  • To create and manage your account and your business.
  • To handle bookings, deposits, reminders and notices.
  • To share with the business you choose what it needs to serve you (name, mobile, service, date, health insurer if you give it).
  • To manage the Fluye Pro and Verified Plus subscriptions of those who activate them.
  • Security, fraud prevention and legal compliance.

3. Who we share data with

With the business you book with, and with providers that serve us under confidentiality agreements: server hosting (Render), push notifications (Google Firebase Cloud Messaging), Google sign-in, email delivery (Resend) and subscription payments (Paddle.com, which acts as merchant of record and processes your payment data as an independent controller under its own privacy policy). WhatsApp messages are sent by you from your own WhatsApp app.

4. Payments

Deposit transfers go from your bank to the business’s account. Subscription payments are processed by Paddle.com as merchant of record: Paddle receives your name, email, country and payment method to charge you, issue the receipt, calculate taxes and prevent fraud. Fluye only receives from Paddle the status of your subscription and payment; we never receive or store card numbers.

Payments processed by Paddle. Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle provides all customer service inquiries and handles returns.

5. Retention

We keep your data while you have an active account. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data; the business keeps an anonymous record of the appointments it served for accounting reasons. Technical security logs are kept for a limited time.

6. Security

We use HTTPS, bcrypt-hashed passwords, revocable sessions, rate limits and role-based access controls. No system is infallible; if we detect an incident that affects you, we will tell you.

7. Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data in the app (Profile → Edit and Profile → Delete my account), through the support chat in the app or by emailing santiagowarmi8@gmail.com. We reply within 10 business days.

8. Minors

Fluye is intended for people 18 and older. An adult can book for minors in their care with the “Someone else” option.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post it here and announce it in the app before it takes effect.

10. Controller and contact

Email: santiagowarmi8@gmail.com · Support: live chat in the app (Profile → Help) · Web: appfluye.app
